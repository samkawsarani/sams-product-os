## Tips

- **Also heard as** lists how transcripts and notes garble the term (e.g. "Nimbus" heard as "nimble us"). Meeting skills use it to correct notes.
- **Defined in** links to the source of truth when one exists, e.g. a metric definition in `knowledge/product-analytics/`. Keep the full definition there, not here.
- Terms only. People go in `knowledge/people/`.
- Start with 3 to 5 terms you use daily. Wrap-up proposes new ones as they come up.
